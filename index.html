FAQ

Pertanyaan yang Sering Ditanyakan

Temukan jawaban untuk pertanyaan umum seputar produk dan layanan kami

How long does tiramisu last?
Our tiramisu stays fresh for 3-4 days when properly refrigerated. We recommend consuming within 48 hours for the best taste and texture.
Do you deliver nationwide?
Yes! We offer nationwide delivery with special temperature-controlled packaging to ensure your tiramisu arrives fresh and perfect.
Can I customize my order?
Absolutely! We offer custom orders for special occasions. Contact us directly to discuss your requirements.
Is there alcohol in your tiramisu?
Traditional tiramisu contains Marsala wine. We also offer alcohol-free versions upon request.
What are your payment options?
We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and popular digital wallets like GoPay, OVO, and Dana.
